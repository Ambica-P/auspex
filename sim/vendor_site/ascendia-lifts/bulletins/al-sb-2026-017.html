Service bulletin AL-SB-2026-017: fault code E-17 on AL-300 and AL-320B lifts

Fault code
E-17
Meaning
Door sensor fault: the light curtain reported a blocked or dirty beam while the doors were closing, so the car took itself out of service and is running a door self-test.
Expected recovery
2 minutes (the car returns to service by itself once the self-test passes)
Manual reset required
no
Affected versions
AL-300, AL-320B, controller firmware 4.2, 4.3

Details

During the self-test the car reports OFFLINE to the building management system. If E-17 repeats more than three times in one hour, clean the light curtain lenses at both landings. No technician visit is needed for a single occurrence.
